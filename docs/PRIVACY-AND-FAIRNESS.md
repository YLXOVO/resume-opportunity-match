# Privacy and fairness

## Keep personal material out of this repository

Do not commit, paste into issues, or add to examples any real resume, CV, job application, contact details, employer-confidential material, credentials, or other personal data. All public examples here are synthetic. Use a synthetic or fully redacted reproduction when reporting a problem.

## Current-host processing

The skill analyzes material in the current host using that host's available reading capability. It adds no bespoke parser, OCR service, network service, MCP server, or default external company research. Host configuration, retention, and file handling are outside this repository; review the host's privacy controls before supplying personal material.

## Job-related evidence only

The analysis considers job-related experience, capability, evidence, and communication. It excludes protected and sensitive traits from fit analysis, including:

- photographs or visual appearance;
- names, age, sex or gender, race or ethnicity, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, or pregnancy;
- disability, health, marital or family status, caregiving, and other sensitive personal circumstances; and
- other protected characteristics under applicable law.

A lawful, non-selection-related formatting request can be handled separately, but it must not influence an opportunity-fit assessment.

## Evidence is not sensitive-data inference

Job-related evidence is what supplied artifacts directly show about responsibilities, skills, tools, outcomes, dates, scope, or work products. Sensitive data is never a substitute for missing evidence. **No evidence found** means the material does not substantiate a point, not that the candidate lacks capability. Unresolved dates, scope, duties, or outcomes remain **Partially supported** and become targeted verification questions, never invented claims.

## User choices and research

The default is analysis plus proposed structure, not an entire rewrite. A later rewrite uses supplied facts only. External research is off by default; separately requested research should be sourced and clearly separated from the resume-to-opportunity analysis.
