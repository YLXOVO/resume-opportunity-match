# Synthetic opportunity — Product Operations Manager

> **Fictional demonstration only.** OrbitFlow and this opportunity are invented. This is not a live vacancy or a claim about an employer.

OrbitFlow is a fictional B2B SaaS company hiring a Product Operations Manager to make launches repeatable as its product and go-to-market teams grow.

## Responsibilities

- Design end-to-end launch processes: intake, decisions, handoffs, and retrospectives.
- Align Product, Sales, Support, Engineering, and Enablement around launch plans and operating rhythms.
- Use SQL or comparable analysis to diagnose adoption patterns and launch friction.
- Define, monitor, and communicate adoption metrics.
- Create durable internal documentation and train go-to-market teams on releases and feedback loops.
- Coordinate cross-functional delivery, resolve dependencies, and make ownership visible.

## Requirements

- Three years of product operations, business operations, or closely related experience.
- Process design and stakeholder management.
- SQL literacy and comfort interpreting adoption data.
- Clear documentation habits and cross-functional delivery experience.
